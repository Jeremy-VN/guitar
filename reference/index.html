2. Octave shapes

The same note an octave higher, by moving towards the thinner strings. These are the shapes that let you derive the whole board from the two bass strings rather than memorising 72 positions.

MoveThenString pairs it works on
2 thinner+2 frets5→3, 6→4
2 thinner+3 frets3→1, 4→2
3 thinner-3 frets6→3
3 thinner-2 frets4→1, 5→2

The two that matter most: up 2 strings and up 2 frets (works from string 6 and 5), and up 3 strings and down 3 frets (works from string 6 and 5). The B string breaks both — anything crossing G→B shifts by one extra fret, which is why the table above splits the way it does.
